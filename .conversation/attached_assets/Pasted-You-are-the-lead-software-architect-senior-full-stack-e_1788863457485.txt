1. PRODUCT DEFINITION
============================================================

SignalForge is a multi-tenant trading intelligence and automation SaaS platform.

The platform connects users' supported trading accounts/brokers, signal sources, AI signal-processing systems, risk-management engines, trading automation, provider/trader marketplaces, analytics, referrals, subscriptions, KYC, wallets, notifications, support, and administrative/business intelligence systems into one unified platform.

SignalForge should NOT be built as a simple dashboard, demo, landing page, or MVP.

Build the application as a serious production-oriented SaaS foundation with:

- Real authentication

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE:

Referral rewards are NOT deducted from the referred user's trading profits.

The reward is funded from SignalForge's platform revenue.

The reward calculation is based on the referred user's monthly platform/trading earnings metric as configured by the business.

Example configurable rule:

If referred user's eligible monthly earnings = $3,000

Referral reward rate = 0.1%

Referral reward:

$3,000 × 0.1% = $3

The platform funds this reward from its own revenue allocation.

DO NOT take money from the referred user's trading account to pay the referrer.
